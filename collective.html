OUR POSITION

Architecture That Endures.

Founded in Pune by Karan Malhotra, The Social Kollab operates on a foundational architectural principle: digital flagships should possess the permanence, typographic balance, and tactile clarity of physical structures.

In an era of disposable digital interfaces, genuine distinction requires unhurried craft. The internet is crowded with interchangeable templates and superficial trends that erode brand credibility. We take on a maximum of three clients per quarter to ensure direct founding partner direction on every line of code and every typographic token.

The Social Kollab was founded on the opposite conviction: When everyone has access to the same synthetic shortcuts, real commercial value accrues to the brands with unmistakable human craft, mathematical elegance, and conversion-hardened engineering.

Every layout we author is designed around human cognitive psychology. Every line of CSS adheres to strict architectural tokens. Every WebGL shader is handwritten to reflect physical materials—paper, slate, woven fabric, solar caustics.
