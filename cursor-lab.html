Typographic Ergonomics Test

Why Generic AI Cursors Fail Ergonomics

The ubiquitous "dot and trailing hollow ring" was popularized by design templates in 2021. Today, it has become an instant tell of AI-generated boilerplate. Because the hollow ring continuously floats behind the pointer, it overlaps and obscures body paragraphs, creates visual motion sickness, and lags noticeably on 60Hz and 120Hz displays.

A Principal-grade studio cursor respects the reader's eye: it either remains the pristine native OS pointer during editorial reading and only surfaces as a high-tactile badge over interactive media, or provides razor-sharp architectural CAD alignment without floating distractions.
