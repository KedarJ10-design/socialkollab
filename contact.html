Scope & Fit

What kinds of businesses does TheSocialKollab work with?

We work with product brands, manufacturers, medical education platforms, regional media, and advisory firms across Pune and beyond—any team that needs a clear, reliable website grounded in their actual business.

What services are included versus out of scope?

We focus strictly on the four disciplines we have delivered in live commercial production: web design and development, e-commerce storefronts, corporate/institutional websites, and content or academic platforms.

INR Pricing

How are projects scoped and priced?

Every engagement is scoped after reviewing your pages, catalog size, and timeline requirements. Foundation sites start from ₹1.5L and larger e-commerce or platform builds start from ₹3.5L, confirmed as a fixed quote in INR before work begins.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance without forcing a retainer?

Yes. Ongoing studio support for speed updates, seasonal catalog refreshes, or new landing pages is optional and scoped quarterly in INR only when your team requests it.

Code Ownership

Do we own the website code, domain, and content after launch?

Yes. Once the project is handed over, your business owns 100% of the domain, hosting accounts, source code, and digital assets with zero lock-in.

Can another developer maintain the codebase later?

Always. We write clean, semantic HTML, tokenized CSS, and standard ES modules with zero proprietary lock-in so any competent engineer can extend the site effortlessly.

Timeline & Assets

How do we get started if our copy or product photos are not ready?

Send us a short note covering what your business does and which existing sites feel awkward today. We help map the page structure first so your team only writes and photographs what the site genuinely needs.

What is the typical build timeline from kickoff to launch?

Foundation company and advisory sites typically ship in 2 to 4 weeks, while larger e-commerce storefronts and academic platforms take 4 to 8 weeks depending on catalog depth.
